Refund & Cancellation Policy

Standard booking terms, rules, and reimbursement guides for SmartSlot reservations.

1

Cancellations More Than 24 Hours in Advance

If you cancel your booking more than 24 hours prior to the scheduled start time, the booking status will be updated to Cancelled, your payment status will be updated to Refunded, and you will receive a full refund processed back to your payment source within 3 to 5 business days.

2

Late Cancellations (Within 24 Hours)

If you cancel your booking within 24 hours of the scheduled start time, the slot is freed and marked as Cancelled, but no refund will be issued. We enforce this to protect resource providers from late-stage empty slots.

3

Cancellation by Resource Provider / Organisation

If the hosting Organisation cancels the booking or marks the resource as unavailable, the booking will be immediately Cancelled, a full refund will be initiated, and an automated email apology including complete payment refund details will be sent to the user.

4

Free Resource Bookings

For any free resources (price = MWK 0), booking cancellations do not involve any payment operations. The booking status will simply be set to Cancelled and the slot will be immediately freed for other users.

5

No Shows

If a user fails to attend their reserved slot, an administrator can mark the booking status as No Show. Once marked, no refunds are available for that transaction.
